About Weller Logs

Built by Ryan Weller. A free, open well-log viewer for quick correlation and zone petrophysics in the browser. I built it because opening a LAS file and hanging a cross section shouldn't need a license server. Your files stay on your machine.

Built with

  • D3 and Leaflet; USGS National Map basemaps
  • Curve conventions from petroplots
  • Example logs: WOGCC public records via 5minutesofpython; Rw from USGS produced-water data

    Type a name, press Pick, click the depth; or drag a top line, or type its MD. The swatch sets that top's color in every well. A tops CSV (well, top, md) opens with Open.

    BetaMudlogs hang in measured depth (MD), in feet. Subsea is elevation − MD, so it assumes a vertical hole; no survey is applied. Images stay on your computer: in this browser, and inside the project file when you save.
    Calibrate: click a ruled depth line, type its MD. Ctrl or Cmd + scroll zooms.

    Summary by zone

    Zones run from each top to the next top, or to TD. Means are depth-weighted; columns marked g use the geometric mean (resistivity, gas, perm). Hover a value for P10, P50, P90 and null count.

    Edit track

    Vshale comes from GR, or from SP in wells without GR (set in the Interpretation panel). Where the well has a PE log, PE separates carbonate, marl and clean sand. Rules run top to bottom; the first that fits wins.
